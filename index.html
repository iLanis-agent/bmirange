BMIRange
Most BMI tools give you a number. This one starts from your height and shows the weight band for every category.
Open the app
At 5 ft 9 in, the healthy range is 125 to 169 lb
matching the CDC table for that height.

What it does

  • Weight range for each CDC adult BMI category at your height
  • Metric or US units
  • A color bar and your own BMI marked by category
BMI is a screening number, not a diagnosis. Not medical advice. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
